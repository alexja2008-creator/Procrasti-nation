# ProcrastiNation — Beta Feedback Form (v2, TestFlight)

> Copy these questions into a Google Form (or Typeform / Notion).
> **Who:** the 10 students on TestFlight (the iPhone app, on production).
> **When:** after about a week with the app (5 days at least), so their first week, from the Oath's plan to coming back, has happened.
> **Bugs go through TestFlight,** not this form: take a screenshot in the app, then Share Beta Feedback. This form is about the experience.
> **Target:** all 10. The results feed `business-marketing-plan.md` (section 8) and the go / no-go for the App Store and the content push.

---

## Suggested Form Header

**Title:** Help us build something you'll actually use.

**Description:**
> You're one of the first citizens of ProcrastiNation, so your answers decide what we build next. 13 questions, about 5 minutes, most of them one tap. Be honest: "I stopped using it" is the most useful thing you can tell us.

---

## The Questions

---

### Q1 — The First Few Minutes
**"Was there a moment in your first few minutes (the questions at the start, your first plan, saving your passport) where you almost quit or got confused? What happened?"**

*Type: Paragraph (open text)*

*Why we ask: The Citizenship Application is our funnel, and it gets longer once the trial page joins it. Every drop-off here costs a sign-up from the content push. Even "nothing" is useful data.*

---

### Q2 — Did It Get You Started?
**"Did ProcrastiNation help you actually start something you'd been putting off? What happened?"**

*Type: Paragraph (open text)*

*Why we ask: The single most important question. v2's promise is starting, not finishing; we need to know if it works, in their own words.*

---

### Q3 — Where It Lost You
**"After the first day, was there a moment you got confused, frustrated, or just stopped opening the app? What happened?"**

*Type: Paragraph (open text)*

*Why we ask: Surfaces the confusing flows and the reasons people drift away, which the usage data shows only as silence.*

---

### Q4 — What You'd Miss
**"If one part of ProcrastiNation disappeared tomorrow, which would you miss most?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- Plan it (breaking a task into small steps)
- Start Mode (the five-minute timer, and "I'm stuck")
- Reminders
- Declarations (signing a start time)
- The Morning Briefing
- Territories and Notes
- The Passport (stamps and ranks)
- None of it

*Why we ask: What we actually use is already in the database; what people value isn't. This tells us what to lead with on the landing page and in videos.*

---

### Q5 — Your Nudges
**"Which nudge tone did you pick?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- Diplomat
- Drill Sergeant
- Roast
- I don't remember

**Follow-up: "How did the reminders land?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- One got me to start something
- Helpful, but I didn't act on them
- Annoying, so I turned them off
- I never got any

*Why we ask: Reminders are what bring people back, and the tones are our best content hook. "Turned them off" means the tone or the timing is wrong.*

---

### Q6 — Worth Sharing
**"Did you screenshot or send anything from ProcrastiNation to someone (a reminder, a plan, a stamp)? If so, what?"**

*Type: Short answer (optional)*

*Why we ask: Whatever students share on their own is what our TikToks and Reels should show.*

---

### Q7 — The "Tell a Friend" Test
**"On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend ProcrastiNation to a friend who struggles with procrastination?"**

*Type: Linear scale (0 = not at all, 10 = already texted them)*

**Follow-up (show regardless of score):**
**"What's the main reason for that score?"**

*Type: Paragraph (open text)*

*Why we ask: This is NPS, and word of mouth is half our growth plan. The follow-up is where the gold is: it tells us exactly what drives or kills word of mouth.*

---

### Q8 — Willingness to Pay
**"Which of these would make ProcrastiNation worth $4.99/month to you?"**

*Type: Checkboxes — pick up to 2*

Options:
- More than 2 AI plans a month (what Pro gives you today)
- A Monday email with your week and what's next
- Import my syllabus and get every assignment planned
- Task templates for common situations (studying, job hunting, projects)
- A check-in halfway through a plan that adjusts the rest
- Honestly, I'd pay for what's already here
- I wouldn't pay for any version of this

**Follow-up (optional): "Anything else that would make it worth paying for?"**

*Type: Paragraph (open text)*

*Why we ask: Ranks the Pro roadmap against what's built, and the follow-up catches what we didn't think to list. The Monday email is State of the Union (planned, not built). Daily nudges are already in v2 (reminders and the morning list), and v1's co-working sessions and streak freeze are gone, so none of them are options.*

---

### Q9 — Monthly or Yearly
**"If you paid, which would you pick?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- $4.99 a month
- $29.99 a year (save 50%)
- Neither

*Why we ask: Settles the open question of whether the trial page leads with yearly, and checks the funnel model's guess that ~70% of payers pick yearly.*

---

### Q10 — Paying for Apps
**"How do you usually pay for an app or subscription?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- With my own card or Apple ID
- A parent or guardian approves it (Ask to Buy, a family plan)
- I don't have a way to pay right now

*Why we ask: The required trial takes a payment method up front. If many students can't add one, we test the reverse trial (Pro with no payment method, then free) early.*

---

### Q11 — Who Are You?
**"Which of these best describes you?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- High school student
- College or grad student
- Working (0–5 years in)
- Working (5+ years in)
- Freelancer or entrepreneur
- Other

**Follow-up (optional): "Do you have ADHD or something similar (diagnosed or not)?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — Yes / No / Prefer not to say*

*Why we ask: Validates our ICP (students, especially with ADHD). The follow-up is optional because it's health information.*

---

### Q12 — Can We Quote You?
**"If you wrote something above that we'd like to share (on our site or in a video), can we quote you?"**

*Type: Multiple choice — pick one*

Options:
- Yes, with my first name
- Yes, but anonymously
- No

*Why we ask: We only use real quotes, with permission (the FTC's rule on reviews, and our own "true numbers only").*

---

### Q13 — The Open Floor
**"Anything else you want us to know? Feature ideas, complaints, things you loved — all welcome."**

*Type: Paragraph (open text, optional)*

*Why we ask: Some of the best product insights come from the things people say when they're not being asked a specific question.*

## What to Do With Results

**After 5 responses:**
- Any Q1 or Q3 friction mentioned by 2+ people → fix it before the App Store
- Note what Q6 says people shared → turn it into the first content hooks

**After 10 responses:**
- **NPS (Q7)** = % who gave 9–10 minus % who gave 0–6. The business plan's target is above 50.
- **Q4 and Q5:** lead the landing page and the videos with what people would miss most and the tone they picked
- **Q8:** the most-picked option → validate against the roadmap's Next list; add any suggestion 2+ people wrote in the follow-up to the roadmap's ideas
- **Q9:** the yearly share → decide whether the trial page leads with yearly, and update the funnel model
- **Q10:** if 3+ of 10 can't pay on their own → plan the reverse-trial test for launch, not later
- **Q11:** the dominant user type → sharpen the ICP
- Pull the 2–3 most compelling Q2 quotes **from people who said yes in Q12** → testimonials on the landing page
- Review Q13 for unexpected insights

**Decision gate:**
- **Go** (the App Store, then the content push): at least 4 of 10 say it got them to start something in Q2, AND NPS ≥ 30
- **Fix first, then re-survey:** anything less. Start with whatever Q1 and Q3 say stopped people.
