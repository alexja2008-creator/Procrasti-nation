# TestFlight: What to Test

The notes on the latest TestFlight build, pasted into App Store Connect → TestFlight → the build → Test Details → What to Test (4,000 characters at most). Written for someone opening PN for the first time, with a short "New in this build" at the end for returning testers. Each new build: update that section from `CHANGELOG.md`, check the rest still matches the app, and replace this copy.

**Build 11 (2026-10-20).** Alex's notes from build 10, with the + sheet's Start choices, "Declare a start time?", the row menu, the Morning Briefing and the ? guide added.

```text
Welcome to ProcrastiNation, and thanks for being one of our first citizens.

ProcrastiNation helps you start the things you've been putting off. Tell it what you're avoiding, tap "Plan it" to break it into steps small enough to begin, then start one with a timer.

THE ONE THING WE'D LOVE YOU TO TRY
Pick something real you've been avoiding this week (an essay, studying for a test, an application, your room) and use the app to get started on it.

WHAT TO TRY
- The Application: a few quick questions so the app fits how you work, about 3 minutes. Save your passport at the end so nothing gets lost (open the email link on this iPhone).
- Add a task with the + button. Type it the way you'd say it: "chem lab report due friday", "gym tue/thu 6pm", "pay rent every 1st". Pick when you'll start (Today, Tomorrow, Pick a date or Later). A due date is optional.
- Tap "Plan it" on a big task. Answer its questions (or skip them) and see if the steps make sense.
- Start a step. The timer counts down from the step's estimate, and "I'm stuck" gives you a fresh two minutes.
- Tap "Declare a start time?" beside a task on Today: pick when you'll begin and sign, and the app nudges you then. Start on time and it's a Declaration kept, stamped in your Passport. Allow notifications when it asks.
- Press and hold any task for Start, Plan it, Move to…, Rename… and File in….
- Check Today each morning (the Morning Briefing helps sort anything that carried over), and Upcoming for the week ahead.
- New words like Customs and Territories? Tap the ? beside the magnifier on Today.

WHAT WE WANT TO HEAR
- Did it help you start something you'd been avoiding?
- Anything confusing, slow or broken, especially picking dates.
- Plans that felt too big, too small, or just off.
- Anything that made you feel judged. That's a bug to us.

HOW TO SEND FEEDBACK
Take a screenshot in the app and tap "Share Beta Feedback", or open TestFlight and tap "Send Beta Feedback". You can also email procrasti.nation.marketing@gmail.com. We'll send a short survey after about a week.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Plan it is free during the beta (up to 20 plans a day).
- Your account works on the web too: app.procrasti-nation.work
- This is an early build. If something breaks, it's not you.

NEW IN THIS BUILD (if you've tested before)
- "Declare a start time?" now sits beside tasks on Today and on the Up next card, so you don't have to press and hold to sign one.
```
