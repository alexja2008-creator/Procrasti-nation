# Changelog

What changed for people using PN, newest first, in their words. One entry per release (a web deploy or a TestFlight build). It feeds TestFlight's "What to Test" notes and release posts. Git has the technical detail.

## Unreleased

- "When" is now **Start**: the day you'll start something. Adding a task asks for it with Today, Tomorrow, Pick a date or Later (or just press Enter, as before), and a due date is optional, behind Add a due date. Give something a due date and PN picks a start day before it, the day before or today for something big, which you can change before adding.
- Start Mode's timer follows a step's estimate: a 30-minute step counts down from 30:00, and a plan's first step stays a few minutes long. A task on its own still gets the length set in Settings → Start Mode, and a later step with no estimate gets 25 minutes.
- Plan it's questions: the answer boxes wrap and grow to show everything you type, and the page scrolls down to Build my plan. Return moves to the next question, and on the last one it puts the keyboard away. On a laptop, Shift+Return starts a new line.
- Show steps on Today keeps a plan's finished steps in the list, struck through, with when you did them, so you can see where you left off. Its count is what's left for today.
- New here? The ? beside the magnifier on Today, Upcoming and Territories (or Settings → Help) opens How to use ProcrastiNation: what Customs, Territories, plans, steps, notes, Declarations, Start Mode, the Morning Briefing and stamps mean, and how to capture, plan, start and repeat things.
- Upcoming shows every turn of a repeat in the week ahead: "bio lab every Mon and Wed" now shows Wednesday too. The next turn is checkable as before; the later ones are marked "Repeats · opens after Mon", and tapping their repeat mark says, right there, when they open.
- Rename a task from its page (tap the button beside the title) or with Rename… in a row's menu.
- Repeat → Pick days… lets you choose the days (Mon, Wed and Sat, say) and change them later. The task moves to the first of the new days.
- Move a plan's step to a later day and PN asks "Move the rest of the plan too?". Move them keeps the steps after it in order, never past the deadline, with Undo.
- A task set for a later day gets its plan starting on that day, not today.
- Picking a day is Today, Tomorrow or Pick a date (a calendar) everywhere, the Declaration included. "This weekend" and "Next week" are gone, and the Application's "When is it due?" no longer starts on "In a week".
- Other time is easy to pick on a laptop: tap an hour, then :00, :15, :30 or :45, with no sideways scrolling. On iPhone it's iOS's own time wheel.
- Adding a task has Due · Today · Tomorrow · Pick a date under the box, so you can give it a deadline without typing one. Enter still adds it straight away.
- Repeats understand the way people say them: "walk the dog on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays", "every M/W/Sat", "gym tue/thu", "take vitamins daily 8am". The task starts on the first of those days, and the sheet says so ("Starts Sat").
- A repeat without a time says that adding one ("6pm") gets you a nudge each time. Plan it isn't offered for repeats when you add them (as everywhere else), since a plan is for a one-off.
- Save your passport asks for your email first, and the username fills in from it (change it if you like). An email typed into the username box moves to the email box whole.
- During the beta, Plan it has no monthly limit, through November 15 (up to 20 plans a day still applies), and plans made during the beta don't use up the free plans after it.
- (Repo only) Docs and Claude Code setup reorganized; no change to the app.

## 2026-10-15: Saving your passport

- Save your passport is now the last step of the Citizenship Application, with no Later: a passport that isn't saved lives in one browser and is lost if that browser is cleared. Sending the email link is enough to carry on; open it whenever you like.
- Passports started before today that were never saved ask to be saved the next time you open the app.
- Until you open the link, Today, Passport and Settings show where it went, with Send it again and Use a different email.
- If your email already has a passport, you can sign in to that one instead.
- The privacy policy and terms say saving comes before continuing (revised October 15).

## 2026-10-14: ProcrastiNation 2.0 on the web

- A new app at app.procrasti-nation.work, replacing v1. Everyone starts fresh through the Citizenship Application.
- Plan it breaks a big task into small, dated steps; Start Mode gets you going with a short timer and "I'm stuck"; Declarations let you sign a start time.
- Today, Upcoming, Territories, Notes and Search; quick add reads things like "fri 6pm #chem".
- Reminders by web push in the tone you picked (Diplomat, Drill Sergeant or Roast), a morning list, and the Morning Briefing for what carried over.
- The Passport: stamps and ranks for starting.
- Free: 2 AI plans per month-long window, and "I'm stuck" for everyone.
- The Citizenship Application asks what to call you, right after Welcome. You can change it in Settings → Name.
- Every day ends at midnight unless you pick a later end in Settings → Day ends at. Night owls no longer see yesterday's Today after midnight.
- A plan is one row on Today, showing how far along it is ("2/7"), with Show steps to open it. Start on a plan starts its next step, and a plan with every step done asks before it's marked done.
- The Application asks for your date of birth, checked on your device and never kept: PN is for 13 and older.
- The site's fonts load from procrasti-nation.work itself, not from Google. The privacy policy and terms were revised the same day.
