## 2026-10-14: ProcrastiNation 2.0 on the web

- A new app at app.procrasti-nation.work, replacing v1. Everyone starts fresh through the Citizenship Application.
- Plan it breaks a big task into small, dated steps; Start Mode gets you going with a short timer and "I'm stuck"; Declarations let you sign a start time.
- Today, Upcoming, Territories, Notes and Search; quick add reads things like "fri 6pm #chem".
- Reminders by web push in the tone you picked (Diplomat, Drill Sergeant or Roast), a morning list, and the Morning Briefing for what carried over.
- The Passport: stamps and ranks for starting.
- Free: 2 AI plans per month-long window, and "I'm stuck" for everyone.
- The Citizenship Application asks what to call you, right after Welcome. You can change it in Settings → Name.
- Every day ends at midnight unless you pick a later end in Settings → Day ends at. Night owls no longer see yesterday's Today after midnight.
